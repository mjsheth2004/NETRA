Section 1 • Technical Foundation

1. NETRA System Architecture & Data Flow

NETRA is engineered under strict partial observability constraints downstream of a unidirectional data diode or passive optical tap. The system never injects packets, never probes endpoints, and never decrypts TLS payloads.

ONE-WAY / READ-ONLY IP TRAFFIC (Zero TX link)
  ↓
1. PASSIVE INGEST: PCAP Replay / NetFlow / IPFIX / sFlow / Promiscuous Raw Sockets
  ↓
2. FLOW & MICRO-BATCH ENGINE: 5-Tuple flow aggregation into sliding 1–5 second windows
  ↓
3. MULTI-VIEW FEATURE EXTRACTION: Flow & Volume • Inter-Arrival Timing • DNS Metadata • TLS/QUIC JA3/JA4 Fingerprints • Diversity Entropy • Graph Topology • History
  ↓
4. BEHAVIOR INDEX: Normalized compact vector representation of window-level entity activity
  ↓
5. MULTI-MODEL DETECTION: Heuristic Rules + Supervised XGBoost + Isolation Forest + TCN (Planned Research)
  ↓
6. BEHAVIORAL MEMORY: Entity-specific statistical baselines • Current vs Recent vs Historical • Anti-Poisoning Quarantine
  ↓
7. EVIDENCE FUSION & CALIBRATION: Multi-detector evidence synthesis • Compound Risk Score (0–100) • Confidence • Uncertainty
  ↓
8. EXPLAINABLE ALERT: Structured SIH JSON • Natural Language Evidence • TreeSHAP Feature Attribution
  ↓
9. REAL-TIME SOC DASHBOARD: Live alert stream • Forensic drawer • Temporal timeline • Line-rate telemetry

💡 Scientific Integrity Mandate: No Fake ML Numbers or Unsupported Claims

Do not falsely claim that every model is already trained and production-ready. The Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) is part of the NETRA research design, but until trained on real lab sequences, it must be documented as a planned research milestone. Furthermore, do not invent accuracy, precision, recall, throughput, or latency numbers. Any benchmark not yet recorded must display "Not yet measured".
